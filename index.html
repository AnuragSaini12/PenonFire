About The Author

Dr. Uday Dokras is a distinguished historiographer, Consultant in Vaastu & Vedic Astrology and renowned expert in Vedic Mandirs and Buddhist stupas. He holds the #1 global ranking in 10 categories, including fastest writing of scholarly works, highest number of books and research papers, the most publications on Dhamma (Buddhism), and pioneering works in Vedic Architecture and Indo-Chinese historical enigmas such as Dvaravati, Vandan, Prambanan, Angkor Wat, and Borobudur. Currently serving as the Director (Technical) of the Mahakali Mandir project in Bangkok, Thailand, Dean of the International Institute of Historiography, Indo Nordic Author’s Colletive and The DoortoBuddha. Dr. Uday is also a sought-after Vaastu and Temple Design Consultant. His illustrious career spans leadership roles in corporate and academic spheres, including:

• Group Vice-President, MZI Group, New Delhi.

• Head of Human Relations, Go Air and Hotel Holiday Inn.

• HR Leadership, Amusement Park Fun and Food Village (Nagpur & New Delhi).

Professional Fields

His pen has discovered numerious original interpretations and "discoveries" in the following areas such as Vedic Architecture, Mandalas and Cosmology Asian complex mandalas and physical models of the cosmos, aligning their design with celestial movements and Vedic principles. His work documents the historical and cultural links between India and ancient South East Asian kingdoms -Srivijaya, Dvaravati, Suvarnabhumi and the Khmer Empire, often revealing previously overlooked nuances in their architectural and religious evolution.

Reviews of his work note that he has identified or proposed hundreds of "new chronicles,"theories, interpretations and ideas regarding the spread of Hinduism and Buddhism in Indo-China and Southeast Asia and design dissections of ancient structures

His work has garnered a global readership nearing 10 Million. It has been translated into multiple languages. While his productivity is undeniable, this massive output is self-published or available on platforms such as Academia.edu/Scribd and ResearchGate making it accessible to all; in terms of sheer creative energy and volume.
